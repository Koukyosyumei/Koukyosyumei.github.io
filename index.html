About

I am a third-year PhD student in the Computer Science department at Columbia University, advised by Prof. Junfeng Yang. I also work closely with Prof. Suman Jana and am a member of the Center for Digital Finance and Technologies. My current work focuses on Software Security, Zero Knowledge Proofs, and AI agents.

I got a Bachelor of Arts and Science in Informatics from the University of Tokyo, where I was advised by Prof. Alex Fukunaga. During my undergraduate studies, I spent a gap year as a research intern at Tsinghua University, supervised by Prof. Yang Liu and Prof. Jingjing Liu.

I am also passionate about open-source development, and my projects have gained 1.4k+ GitHub stars in total.
